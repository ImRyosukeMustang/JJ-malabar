About the kitchen

A Hillcrest Metro regular for a reason

JJ Malabar Restaurant runs a straightforward, well-worn menu of Kerala-style fries, Manchurian dry preparations, and Malabar biryani, served fast in a casual dining room with easy parking out front.

Reviewers point to the biryani and chicken dishes first, then to the price — most meals land in the ₹200–400 range — and to a dining room that's kept clean even through a lunchtime rush.
