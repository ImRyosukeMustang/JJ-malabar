# JJ Malabar Restaurant

**Indian restaurant · Ooty, Tamil Nadu**
Rating: 4.0 (362 reviews) · ₹200–400 per person

## About

JJ Malabar Restaurant sits inside Hillcrest Metro on Westbury Road in Monterosa
Colony, Ooty — a casual, sit-down spot known for Malabar-style biryani,
Kerala dishes, and a long list of pepper-fry and Manchurian-style sides.
Diners call out the biryani, the chicken dishes, and the mix of Arabian,
Kerala, and North Indian options, alongside reasonable prices, good service,
and easy parking.
